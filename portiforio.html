I'm Severain, a developer who cares about the details.

I've spent the last 5+ years crafting web applications that are fast, accessible, and beautiful. From AI assistants to developer training platforms, I love building tools that solve real problems. Those early days shaped who I am as a developer today — curious, determined, and always eager to learn. The school's focus on science and technology gave me a strong foundation that I still build upon every single day.

When I'm not coding, I'm exploring new frameworks, contributing to open source, or spending time with my cyber_girl gena_rwa. The SS+34 logo you see represents my personal brand — Severain + 34 (my lucky number and the year I committed to becoming a professional developer).
